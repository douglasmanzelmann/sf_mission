If time is short

Prioritize Mission Dolores for history and architecture, Clarion Alley for public art, and Paxton Gate for curiosities.

Optional southward extension

If you still have energy after lunch, continue toward 24th Street for more murals, Latino-owned businesses, and neighborhood food stops. Keep it flexible rather than turning the day into a checklist.
